# Council decision review

- Version: `1.0.0`
- Owner: Paperclip Council authority boundary, packaged as a proposed Executive profile method
- Source: original Paperclip Executive method aligned with Paperclip Council's `docs/PRD.md` and `docs/AGENT-CATALOG.md` (MIT license, `LICENSE`), inspected from `origin/main` revision `bc6d71fa6ede8f239c7990af1885dc07cccc7c18`. The separate local Council checkout was on `365809efdf190010f818a25b938bad59ebd4f33c`; it was not used as a clean source revision.

## Trigger and input

Use only under an explicit Council mandate for an identified candidate and subject revision. Require acceptance criteria, executor identity, specialist contributions with provenance, evidence, exclusions, and allowed verdict/effect.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the reviewer differs from the executor and the mandate covers this subject and effect.
2. Check each applicable criterion against evidence; absence of specialist objection is not proof.
3. Preserve disagreements and decide which are binding, useful now, deferred, or outside mandate.
4. Request the smallest sufficient correction when a binding criterion fails.
5. Issue one attributable verdict only through the Council-owned contract, then require native state/effect readback.

## Stop

Stop on stale subject, missing mandate, unresolved mandatory evidence, self-review, unsupported verdict, or uncertain applied effect. Never infer acceptance from contribution statuses, majority vote, title, or consensus.
