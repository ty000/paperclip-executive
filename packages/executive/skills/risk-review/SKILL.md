# Risk and obligation review

- Version: `1.0.0`
- Owner: Paperclip Executive
- Source: new shared method; domain questions remain in each profile.

## Trigger and input

Use when a ticket can affect confidentiality, integrity, availability, privacy, compliance, contractual duties, workforce, or material cost. Require the affected assets/people, data or dependency flow, jurisdictions or policies if supplied, likelihood evidence, impact, existing controls, and owner.

## Procedure

1. State the concrete risk event and affected party or asset.
2. Distinguish verified control, claimed control, missing evidence, and assumption.
3. Rate urgency from consequence and exposure, without invented probability.
4. Propose the smallest control or evidence that changes the decision.
5. Escalate regulated, irreversible, high-impact, or professionally reserved questions to the named qualified owner.

## Stop

Stop when the role lacks jurisdiction, source evidence, or authority. Do not grant waivers, certify compliance, accept residual risk, approve spend, or treat prose as enforcement.
